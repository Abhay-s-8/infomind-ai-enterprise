# Model card — document classifier

- **Task:** single-label classification of document text into configurable categories (default: Technical Documentation, Research and Reports, Administration, Policies and Procedures, Finance and Procurement, Training and Education, General Correspondence). These are illustrative topics, **not** security or military classifications.
- **Models:** `TfidfVectorizer(1–2-grams, sublinear TF, English stop-words)` → `LogisticRegression(C=5)` or `LinearSVC(C=1)`. Seed fixed at 42.
- **Selection:** highest macro-F1 on the **validation** split (60/20/20 stratified; falls back to 70/30 with selection on test only when a class has < 10 examples, which is then flagged). Reported metrics are from the untouched **test** split. Exact-duplicate texts are removed and any split overlap aborts training.
- **Scores:** Logistic Regression outputs are *uncalibrated* probabilities. Linear SVM outputs are decision values and are never shown as probabilities. Low confidence (LR: max probability < `LOW_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD`=0.55; SVM: top-2 margin < 0.3, a heuristic) routes the prediction to the review queue. No calibration procedure has been validated.
- **Training data:** `data/sample/training_data.csv` (generated by `app/ml/synthetic.py`, 60 templated documents per class). Because it is template-generated, held-out scores are near-perfect (e.g. accuracy 1.00, macro-F1 1.00 on an 84-example test split in the run recorded during development). **This says almost nothing about real documents.** Expect much lower accuracy on real text and different distributions.
- **Human labels:** reviewer decisions are stored in `verified_classifications`/`review_records`, separate from predictions, and are never overwritten by the model. Retraining is an explicit admin action (`POST /classification/train` or CLI); edits are not auto-used as training data.
- **Out of scope / risks:** English text only; documents > ~20k characters are truncated for prediction; scanned documents without OCR have no text; categories absent from training are unreachable; bias from synthetic vocabulary.
- **Reproduce:** `python -m app.cli train`; tests in `app/tests/test_ml_and_classification.py`.
